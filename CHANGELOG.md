# Better Tracking

## Unreleased

- Tracking Alert is now Better Tracking. The command is `/bettertracking`, and `/tra` stays.
- Herb and ore swap: alternates Find Herbs and Find Minerals every 2 or 4 seconds while you move, so you see both on the minimap. It waits in combat, towns, instances, on flights, while standing still, casting or looting, while an enemy is targeted or under your cursor and while another tracking is on.
- If Blizzard blocks timed swaps, the swap runs on your movement keys instead.
- Herbs and ore that a swap reveals ping once, like any other new node.
- A minimap button that shows what you track right now. Left-click opens the settings, middle-click turns the swap on or off, right-click pauses alerts. `/tra minimap` hides or shows it.
- New icon.
- Right-click on the minimap button or the addon menu entry pauses and resumes alerts and keeps your sound and flash choices.
- The minimap button tooltip and `/tra status` show the name of the last node that pinged.
- A first-run chat line says to turn on Find Herbs or Find Minerals and hover a blip once, and the tooltip repeats it until minimap alerts are on.
- Calibration tries two more ways to read the minimap and says once when none of them fits.
- Says once when the minimap stops answering, and the tooltip shows it until /reload.
- Gathering Nodes Only also skips blips that the game names as a creature, player, pet or vehicle.
- No alerts while you are dead or a ghost.
- The GatherMate2 source is now off by default and named Also Ping at Spots GatherMate2 Remembers, because GatherMate2's circles mark remembered spots.
- Node Types lists only the four GatherMate2 types that exist on WoW Forever.
- Removed the GatherMate2 display options (hide icons, merge circles, circle size).
- Removed the tuning options for probes per frame, full sweep, only while moving, the muted-effects channel and flash thickness. Their defaults now always apply.
- Rares and Treasures replaces Also Alert on Vignettes, and chat lines use plain sentences.
- Settings open after combat when you ask for them during a fight.
